---
title: Disclaimer
description: Important information about what OpenGnome is and is not
---

OpenGnome is research tooling. It is not a clinical product.

It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

Confidence and signal strength values are estimates produced over retrieved evidence. They are not statistical guarantees and should not be interpreted as clinical measurements.

Every output should be treated as a starting point for further investigation by a qualified medical professional.

The platform retrieves publicly available research abstracts and trial registrations. The quality of the output depends on the quality and availability of the retrieved evidence.

Do not use OpenGnome to make medical decisions.
