## Current revenue streams (live)

Secondary sale royalty: Every anchored NFT carries 500 bps creator royalty. When a report trades on Magic Eden or Tensor, 5% of the sale price routes to the platform treasury.

This is the only active revenue stream during beta.

## Platform costs covered by the platform during beta

Solana transaction fees for compressed NFT minting.

Arweave storage for image and metadata uploads.

Image generation costs.
